Frequently asked questions

The menu bar didn't turn black. Click the Notchless icon in the menu bar. If it asks for access to your wallpaper, click Allow Access and choose the folder your wallpaper is in (or your Home folder). Aerial and video wallpapers can't be painted on; pick a picture or a dynamic wallpaper.

How do I get my original wallpaper back? Turn off "Hide the notch", or choose Restore Wallpaper & Quit from the icon's right-click menu. macOS only lets apps change the Space in front, so visit your other Spaces once and Notchless restores them as you go.

Another notch app is also running. Two notch hiders replace each other's wallpaper. Quit the other one.

Restore purchases. Settings › Your Plan › Restore Purchases. Purchases follow your Apple Account.

Cancel Pro. System Settings › your name › Media & Purchases › Subscriptions, or the App Store › your account › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
