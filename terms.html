Plans

  • Notchless Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks notch hiding, rounded corners and dynamic wallpaper support for as long as the app is available.
  • Notchless Pro is an auto-renewable monthly subscription that unlocks every feature. If a free trial is offered, it converts to a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless cancelled.

Billing and cancellation

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Your wallpaper

Notchless changes your desktop picture to a painted copy and puts the original back when you turn it off. Keep your own copies of pictures you care about; Notchless never deletes or edits your original files.
